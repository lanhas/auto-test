## Description

As per Compliance request (ref memo CM-2026-031) need to enhance bene nomination in eApp.
Currently customer can only pick Spouse / Parent / Child / Sibling / Others, and for extended
family the FP picks "Others" and types free text --> causing NIGO at NB as relationship is
unclear and no declaration is captured.

As a Financial Planner
I want to choose a more specific relationship for the beneficiary
So that the application is in good order and the compliance declaration is captured upfront

**Scope**
- HK only. All products. Except the ones still on old form template - QA06, SP07 (pls refer to product list in confluence)
- eApp web only (tablet app is separate story POS-2431)

## AC

**AC1** - applicable for all HK products except excluded products. For excluded products no change to current behaviour.

**AC2**
Add below new values to "Relationship to Life Insured" dropdown:
- Grandparent
- Grandchild
- Nephew
- Niece

Sequence: after Sibling, before Others.

**AC3**
GIVEN FP is on Beneficiary page
WHEN one of the new relationship is selected for a beneficiary
THEN system displays declaration section under that beneficiary w/ a checkbox (default unticked)
Wording refer to below.

**AC4** - checkbox is mandatory. If not ticked and click Next --> block and show error msg "Please confirm the declaration to proceed." (in red, under the checkbox). User cannot proceed to next step.

**AC5** Declaration should also be printed in the application form PDF, under the beneficiary section.

**AC6** existing relationships no impact

## Wording (EN)

Title: `Declaration of Relationship`

Checkbox text:
`I/We declare that the beneficiary named above is the {relationship} of the Life Insured and that this nomination is made voluntarily. I/We understand that the Company may request documentary proof of the relationship before any benefit is paid.`

TC wording - pending translation team, will update
